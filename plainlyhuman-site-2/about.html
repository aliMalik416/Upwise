About Us

plainlyHuman started with one simple frustration: money advice is either too complicated or too judgmental.

Calculators tell you a number and leave you to figure out what it means. Finance sites talk down to you. Neither one actually helps.

So we built something different — tools that explain what a number means for your actual life, without jargon, without lectures, and without assuming you did something wrong to end up here.

Life gets messy. The numbers don't have to.

plainlyHuman — tools for real life.
