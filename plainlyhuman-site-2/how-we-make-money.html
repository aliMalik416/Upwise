How We Make Money

We're honest about this because trust is the whole point.

Every core tool on plainlyHuman is free, and always will be. No paywalls on the calculators that matter.

When we do make money, it's through:

  • Affiliate links — when a tool recommends a product or service (like comparing credit cards or estimating renovation materials), some of those links earn us a small commission if you sign up or buy.
  • Ads — on some pages.
  • Optional premium features — down the road, for people who want more than the free tools offer.

The one rule that never changes: what we recommend is based on what fits your numbers — never on which company pays us the most. If a $0-commission option is genuinely the better fit for you, that's what we'll show you.
