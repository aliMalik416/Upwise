Trust & Transparency

How We Make Money

We believe financial and career tools must be transparent about how they operate.

Every core calculator and comparison tool on Upwise is completely free. We do not place key financial tools behind paywalls.

To support ongoing development and host statistical data integrations, Upwise may generate revenue through:

  • Affiliate Partnerships: When a user chooses to explore an external provider (such as a high-interest savings account, credit card, or educational provider), Upwise may earn a referral fee at no cost to you.
  • Sponsorships & Ads: Clearly labeled ethical advertising or sponsored placements.
  • Optional Advanced Features: Future premium capabilities for specialized power users or institutional analytics.
Our Core Promise: Recommendations and calculation models are strictly algorithm-driven based on your numbers—never on commission rates. If a zero-commission option is best for you, that is what Upwise displays.
