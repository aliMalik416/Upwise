Government Data

Statistics Canada

Grocery, inflation, and educational ROI metrics reference official Statistics Canada datasets:

  • Consumer Price Index (CPI): Used to establish regional grocery benchmark price movements.
  • Postsecondary Student Information System (PSIS): Used for post-graduation median earnings 2, 5, and 10 years after completion.

Labor Market

Job Bank Canada

Career paths and wage estimates draw on public employment indicators provided by Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC):

  • Median hourly and annual regional wages.
  • Job outlooks and education/skill prerequisites.
